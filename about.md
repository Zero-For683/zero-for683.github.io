I'm a U.S. Navy veteran and cybersecurity professional with hands-on experience in detection engineering, threat hunting, incident response, and offensive security. I've worked across Windows, Linux, and Active Directory environments, with a particular interest in understanding how systems work, how they're attacked, and how to defend them.

I'm currently pursuing my bachelor's degree in cybersecurity through the SANS Technology Institute.
## Training & Certifications

I hold the **Offensive Security Certified Professional (OSCP)** and **CompTIA Security+** certifications and completed a cybersecurity apprenticeship through RapidAscent.

As part of my bachelor's program at SANS, I've earned my **GIAC Foundational Cybersecurity Technologies (GFACT)** and **GIAC Security Essentials (GSEC)** certifications. I'm currently working toward my **GIAC Certified Incident Handler (GCIH)**.

My training and hands-on work have covered Active Directory security, SIEM-based detection, network forensics, penetration testing, and both offensive and defensive security operations.

## Military Background

My background includes five years of experience working with robotic systems in the U.S. Navy at EODMU-1, using sonar to locate underwater mines and other objects. I planned operations, led missions, and maintained a fleet of robotic systems to support leadership throughout the full detect-to-engage sequence.
